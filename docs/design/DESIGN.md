# AGym Design Direction

Status: **founder-selected visual-system brief (Concept B)**. Active source of truth for original internal creative production; it does not authorize publishing, ad spend, email collection, or full campaign launch.

Companion study: `docs/marketing/reference-study-2026-07-11.md`.

## Brand feeling

AGym is for technical people who train and want the reality of that training to remain usable by their agents.

```text
messy real-world training > user review > confirmed context that travels
```

It should feel:

- athletic, fast, cultural, and internet-native;
- technically fluent without resembling enterprise SaaS;
- authored and opinionated rather than polished stock fitness;
- user-controlled, exact, and portable—not opaque automation;
- more like an editorial performance poster than a fitness ad.

It must not feel like another AI coach, wellness tracker, medical dashboard, supplement brand, gaming product, or generic “AI gradient” startup.

## Founder-selected visual principles

The founder selected Nike Running Club Instagram-post energy and supplied poster/editorial references. The transferable direction is **type-led kinetic editorial**, not a reproduction of any referenced brand or artwork.

1. Typography is a primary image-making tool: oversized, cropped, repeated, overlapping, or interrupted by the image.
2. Use controlled collision between type, object/image, texture, and graphic systems—never a card simply placed on a background.
3. Use a compressed, charged color world of two or three colors. Color sets the atmosphere; it is not merely a status accent.
4. Transform images with original duotone, grain, posterization, pixel translation, hard crop, masking, contour fields, or print-like offset. Never rely on staged AI-stock realism.
5. Signal athletic energy through pace, physical motion, scale, crop, repetition, and tension—not obligatory gym-prop still lifes or influencer physiques.
6. Signal technical context through structured typography, coordinates/counters, progression strings, registration marks, or data-like rhythms—not fake UI, floating cards, or arrow decoration.
7. Maintain clear hierarchy: one big thought, one focal visual/gesture, and compact factual product clarification.

## Primary message territory

The accepted core message remains:

> Stop re-explaining your fitness history to AI.

Supporting phrases may include:

- `Training happened. Now make it usable.`
- `Your AI needs the part that happened after the plan.`
- `Log the set. Keep the context.`
- `From training noise to usable context.`
- `RAW > REVIEWED > READY`

Do not use `AI coach`, body/outcome promises, medical claims, “optimize your body,” or “the future of fitness.”

## Visual system

### Typography

Use an intentional contrast between two type roles:

- **Display:** an original/licensed high-contrast editorial serif or expressive display face. It may be oversized, tightly cropped, stretched, repeated, or optically interrupted. It carries the emotional/poster rhythm.
- **Utility:** a neutral, crisp grotesk/technical sans for explanatory copy, CTA support, coordinates, dates, and factual labels.

Rules:

- Do not use the prior condensed-performance headline as the default identity.
- Keep product facts and disclosures readable in the utility face.
- Decorative microtype must never resemble real product UI or make capability claims.
- Typography should be composed deterministically, not generated inside an image model.

### Palette

The primary system is **Concept B's cream / charcoal / electric-orange palette**. Concept C's night-field palette is a founder-approved secondary exploration input, but only when the B transformation mechanism remains clear. Concept A's palette is not selected as a system, though color may be reconsidered later.

| System | Colors | Intended feeling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary — translation | charcoal `#1A1817`, cream `#F4EFE7`, orange `#FF4B19` | physical, contemporary, clear messy-to-usable transformation |
| Secondary — night field | deep teal `#123C42`, shell `#F1E7D7`, coral `#F07C73` | athletic, rich, editorial; preserve mechanism clarity |
| Historical / optional | charcoal `#101010`, bone `#F2EDE3`, vermilion `#E83A22` | potentially interesting color only; do not restore Concept A's unclear visual system |

Use one atmosphere per creative. Grain/print texture is allowed when it creates depth, not a faux-ripped-paper look.

### Image and object treatment

- Prefer a single original visual gesture: transformed training-adjacent object, cropped motion/silhouette, abstract trace, or graphic field.
- Images should be treated, not simply photographed: duotone, halftone, grain, posterization, pixel distortion, hard masks, or contour/wave fields.
- If an image model is used, generate only an original background/object without text, logo, interface, anatomy, or recognizable third-party equipment. Add all type/marks deterministically afterward.
- Avoid posed faces, smiling stock photography, literal gym bags/notebooks/water bottles, barbell still lifes, chalk clichés, and photorealistic “person using a phone after training” scenes.

### Training legibility requirement

The B mechanism alone can read as generic data tooling. Every campaign visual must therefore contain **at least two** of the following, with one in the primary focal area:

1. a plain-language training anchor in the headline or supporting copy: `training`, `workout`, `set`, `run`, or `session`;
2. a compact, original training-log token such as `SET 04`, `3 × 5`, `SESSION 18`, `RUN / EASY`, or `LOAD / ACTUAL`;
3. a non-branded, non-medical physical cue of exertion or movement: a cropped grip, moving stride, resistance shape, track/lane geometry, or an abstracted rep/interval trace;
4. a visual progression that specifically reads as training reality becoming confirmed context—not merely “messy data becomes clean data.”

Use these as integrated poster elements, not as a fake app screen, literal gym-prop still life, or generic fitness decoration. The visual must make a viewer understand **training happened** before asking them to understand the data transformation.

### Technical mechanism

AGym’s recurring mechanism is an editorial transformation—not a UI card:

```text
RAW > REVIEWED > READY
```

Possible expressions:

- a fragmented/duplicated form resolving into a crisp repeated string;
- a raw mark becoming a counted sequence;
- an abstract motion trace passing through a confirmation state;
- coordinates or structured metadata forming a rhythm around the focal image.

These elements must feel designed into the poster’s structure. No pasted-on arrows, fake dashboards, floating SaaS cards, or legible invented product screens.

### Composition

- Build for the mobile feed first: immediate impact at thumbnail size.
- Use large visual scale and deliberate cropping; do not keep every element safely inside the frame.
- Let type and focal object overlap with purpose.
- One headline/idea per asset. Support copy stays minimal and legible.
- Use empty space strategically, but avoid a conventional “copy on the left / stock photo on the right” layout.
- Export source/background and final type-composited creative separately.

## Founder decision — 2026-07-11

The founder chose **Concept B: distorted-object translation** as AGym's primary visual system because it is the clearest expression of the product mechanism.

- Preserve the B logic: unstructured/fragmented input resolves into confirmed, usable context.
- C is a useful secondary input: its deep-teal/shell/coral palette and abstract motion energy are interesting, but its product meaning is too unclear to be the primary system.
- A is rejected for clarity. Its palette may be reconsidered independently, but do not reuse its bar/rule composition as the visual system.
- The next production step is placement-specific internal creative using B's mechanism, with an optional tightly controlled B-mechanism + C-palette variation. Keep the message test disciplined and do not publish without approval.

## Guardrails

- No third-party logos, campaign names, copyrighted photography/artwork, or direct imitation of a named reference.
- No medical/anatomical/biometric imagery, diagnosis, treatment, injury-rehabilitation, or health-outcome claims.
- No body transformation, weight-loss, physique comparison, unsafe exercise, fake testimonials, fake traction, or product-availability claims.
- AGym is a user-controlled fitness data/context layer, not a coach or advice engine.
- Publishing, ad spend, real-email collection, privacy/disclosure changes, and user outreach require founder approval.

## Asset evolution record

Every new asset records: design-spec version, concept territory, message ID, prompt/source path, finished-export path/dimensions, internal review status, and—only after an approved live test—measured results.

## Superseded work
