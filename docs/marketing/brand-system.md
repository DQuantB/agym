## Brand job

AGym makes messy, real-world training behavior usable as user-confirmed context for an agent.

```text
training happened > user reviews the reality > confirmed context travels forward
```

The brand must feel technical and athletic without becoming generic AI SaaS, an AI coach, a wellness brand, or a medical product.

## Active visual direction

**Concept B — distorted-object translation, with limited C secondary input**

- Preserve a visibly clear transformation from unstructured/fragmented training reality into confirmed, usable context.
- The post is an editorial performance poster first, not a SaaS explainer with a fitness image behind it.
- Make oversized, cropped, repeated, and overlapping typography the primary visual event; use a crisp utility sans only for factual supporting copy.
- Primary palette: cream / charcoal / electric orange. A deep-teal / shell / coral variation is allowed only when it preserves B's transformation clarity.
- Treat original imagery through duotone, grain, posterization, pixel translation, hard crop, masking, or contour fields; do not use staged AI-stock realism.
- Signal athletic energy with pace, motion, repetition, scale, and tension—not literal gym-prop scenes or influencer physiques.
- Make training unmistakable in every asset: use at least two of a plain-language training anchor, a compact training-log token, a non-branded movement/exertion cue, and a training-specific reality-to-context progression. The viewer must understand “training happened” before the data transformation.
- Signal technical context through structured type, coordinates/counters, progression strings, registration marks, or data rhythms—not cards, fake dashboards, or pasted-on arrows.
- Use AGym's recurring mechanism as an editorial phrase: `RAW > REVIEWED > READY`.
- Generated imagery is only the background/object layer. Compose all readable typography, marks, branding, CTA, and required disclosures deterministically.

## Voice

- Clear, practical, technically literate, performance-minded.
- Acknowledge that plans are incomplete without logged reality.
- Speak about user control, confirmation, context, and portability.
- Avoid hype, bro-fitness, wellness clichés, generic SaaS vocabulary, and outcome claims.

Preferred visual message territory:

- `Stop re-explaining your fitness history to AI.`
- `Training happened. Now make it usable.`
- `Your AI needs the part that happened after the plan.`
- `Log the set. Keep the context.`
- `RAW > REVIEWED > READY`

## Prohibited territory

- Anime, manga, comic-panel visual language, cartoon athletes, generic floating cards, fake dashboards, or readable fake UI.
- Staged AI-generated stock-photo realism, paper/ripped-paper collage, literal gym-prop still lifes, or pasted-on arrows/context cards.
- Doctors, anatomy, biometrics, clinical imagery, diagnoses, treatment claims, before/after, body transformation, weight loss, or physique comparison.
- Robots, AI trainers, third-party logos, fake testimonials, fake user counts, or product-availability claims.
