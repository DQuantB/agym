# Coach Discovery Operator Guide v1

Status: internal operating guide. Created 2026-08-01. Supports `experiments/2026-08-01-coach-discovery-sprint-v1.md`. It does not authorize automated collection or handling identifiable client data. As of 2026-08-02 the founder has begun sending discovery outreach; a founder-approved `/coaches` landing page exists as an explainer/early-access page and may be shared once a coach confirms relevant pain, per the sending protocol below — do not lead with it.

## Objective

Create a small, high-quality queue of independent online strength/hypertrophy coaches who plausibly experience repeated messy client-check-in work. The purpose is to find discovery conversations, not to maximize lead volume or sell AGym.

## The unit of work

One research batch is 60 minutes:

1. Find 10–15 public candidates.
2. Qualify only those with visible evidence of the first ICP.
3. Record no more than five strong prospects in the founder-review queue.
4. Draft no messages until the prospect row has a public URL and a specific relevance cue.
5. Founder approves, edits, or skips every individual message.

Stop after five approved/sendable candidates. Quality matters more than a long list.

## Public scan sources and search patterns

Use only public pages and public search results. Do not log in, join groups, use a private community, scrape, buy lists, or collect emails/phone numbers.

### 1. Instagram / YouTube / TikTok coach content

Look for coaches whose public content makes online-client check-ins or programming visible.

Search patterns:

```text
"online strength coach" "weekly check in"
"online hypertrophy coach" clients
"online coach" "check-in form" strength
"online fitness coach" "client check-ins"
site:instagram.com "online coach" "check in"
site:youtube.com "online coaching check ins" strength
```

Good public cues:

- explicitly accepts online coaching clients;
- discusses weekly check-ins, client updates, plan adjustments, adherence, or feedback;
- serves general strength/hypertrophy clients rather than only elite athletes or medical rehabilitation;
- has a public profile/site that names a normal contact path.

### 2. Coaching platforms, newsletters, podcasts, and public blogs

Search for coaches who describe their own operations or interview guests about client management.

```text
"online coach" "weekly check-in" Trainerize
"online strength coach" Everfit check-in
"strength coach" "client check-ins" podcast
"hypertrophy coach" online coaching applications
"online coaching" "plan adjustments" strength coach
```

Do not treat a platform's generic marketing page as proof that any named coach uses the workflow. Prospect evidence must be tied to the named coach's public materials.

### 3. LinkedIn and X public posts

Search for operators describing coaching capacity, check-ins, adherence, client communication, admin load, or AI experimentation.

```text
site:linkedin.com/in "online strength coach"
site:linkedin.com/posts "online coach" "weekly check-in"
site:x.com "online strength coach" "check in"
site:x.com coach "ChatGPT" programming
```

Use a public post only as a relevance cue. Do not infer client count, professional qualification, medical role, or LLM use unless the coach states it publicly.

### 4. Communities — research rules first

Potential future sources include coach-focused Discords, subreddits, Facebook groups, Trainerize/Everfit communities, and local trainer networks. At this stage, record only public rules and public threads.

Rate each source:

- `GO`: rules clearly permit a founder to ask a non-promotional workflow question or reply when relevant.
- `ASK`: rules are unclear; founder must contact a moderator before any mention of the research.
- `NO`: promotion/research outreach is disallowed, or the community is private/login-only.

Never use a community rating to bypass individual-contact approval.

## Qualification rubric

Score every candidate before asking the founder to review them.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Online coach fit | unclear / not a coach | coach but remote model unclear | explicit online strength/hypertrophy coaching |
| Recurring check-in evidence | none | generic client support | explicit updates, check-ins, plan adjustment, or feedback workflow |
| Workflow relevance | no public cue | potentially relevant content | concrete cue about fragmented input, adherence, client context, admin, or AI tooling |
| Reachability | no public path | generic platform/contact page | public DM/contact path and no known rule issue |
| Risk | medical/clinical or unclear consent risk | mixed scope | clearly non-clinical, public evidence only |

Only queue candidates with 7+ points and no `NO` community/platform rule. A high score means “worth founder review,” not “likely to buy.”

## Founder-review queue template

Store the queue as an internal draft. Use public handles/URLs only; do not add email addresses, phone numbers, private notes, or client information.

| ID | Public name / handle | Public source URL | ICP evidence | Check-in / workflow cue | Score | First channel | Draft status | Founder decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| `coach-001` |  |  |  |  |  |  | `not drafted` | `pending` |

`First channel` can only be a public, reasonable path: public reply-first, a public contact form, or a publicly open DM path. It is never a permission to send.

## No-link discovery messages

Use only after the founder approves a specific prospect row. Personalize with an observed public cue; do not make a vague compliment or say “I saw your profile.” Keep it to one question and do not pitch AGym, request client data, send a link, or ask for a call.

### Opener A — check-in reconstruction

```text
Hi <name> — I saw you mention <specific public cue>. I’m researching how online strength coaches handle the messy part of weekly client updates. When a check-in arrives across training, adherence, energy, and notes, what do you usually need to reconstruct before deciding whether to change a plan?
```

### Opener B — workflow / tools

```text
Hi <name> — I’m mapping the workflow behind online coaching check-ins, not pitching a product. In a normal week, where does client context arrive for you, and what part of turning it into a plan decision is still manual or annoying?
```

### Opener C — AI only when publicly relevant

```text
Hi <name> — I noticed your public post about <specific AI/workflow cue>. I’m researching whether coaches use AI for internal preparation. What context do you still have to assemble manually before an AI tool can be useful for a client review?
```

### If the coach replies with pain

```text
That is helpful—thank you. Could you walk me through one recent, anonymized example: what arrived, what you had to interpret or chase, and what you eventually changed (if anything)? Please keep client details anonymous.
```

### If they ask what this is about

```text
I’m exploring a future workflow concept, not a finished product: preserving a client’s original update while making a reviewable briefing that a coach can use in their own process or alongside their preferred AI tools. The coach would remain responsible for interpretation and any plan change. I’m first trying to learn whether the underlying check-in problem is real.
```

If the coach wants something to look at rather than just a description, the `/coaches` page (once live) covers the same concept and is labeled as a prototype concept — only share it after this reply, never as the opener.

## Sending protocol

1. Founder reviews five drafts.
2. Founder sends a maximum of 3–5 individually approved messages per day.
3. No follow-up if the coach does not respond within the initial sprint; do not create a sequence.
4. For a public community: read rules first. On X, prefer a useful reply before a DM. On Reddit, do not mention AGym from a cold account; help first and follow each subreddit's rules.
5. Founder records the outcome as `sent`, `replied`, `conversation`, `declined`, or `no response` without pasting sensitive details.
6. Conversation Analyst updates the anonymized evidence ledger on the same day.

## What not to do

- no bulk DMs, sequencers, browser automation, scraping, or bot comments;
- no “I built a tool for coaches” opener;
- no product link before relevant pain is confirmed and a later follow-up is founder-approved;
- no request for screenshots, client logs, health data, or identifiable examples;
- no implication that AGym has real-time monitoring, integrations, a coach dashboard, AI prescriptions, or medical/nutrition capability;
- no public quote, logo, testimonial, or case study without explicit written permission.

## Local workbench

The no-cost local workbench is `scripts/coach-prospect-workbench.mts`. It does not fetch, scrape, log in, or send. It only produces public search queries, validates manually gathered public-source records, applies the rubric, and renders the founder-review queue.

```bash
npm run prospects:queries
npm run prospects:score -- docs/marketing/fixtures/coach-prospect-candidates.example.json
npm run prospects:render -- docs/marketing/fixtures/coach-prospect-candidates.example.json
```

Copy the example JSON to a local, Git-ignored working file before adding real public candidates. Never add private contact details, client information, or sensitive data to a candidate file.
