## Territories

### A — Kinetic type / data rhythm

- Message: `Stop re-explaining your fitness history to AI.`
- Visual mechanism: `CONTEXT` / `REALITY` type is interrupted by bar/rule rhythm and `RAW > REVIEWED > READY`.
- Palette: charcoal, bone, vermilion.
- Role: direct, high-urgency pain-point expression.

### B — Distorted-object translation

- Message: `Training happened. Now make it usable.`
- Visual mechanism: orange pixel fragments and a geometric target/axis system represent unstructured input resolving into confirmed context.
- Palette: cream, charcoal, electric orange.
- Role: clearest visual expression of the product’s messy-to-usable transformation.

### C — Motion-field editorial

- Message: `Your AI needs the part that happened after the plan.`
- Visual mechanism: an abstract original gesture moves through a coral contour field.
- Palette: deep teal, shell, coral.
- Role: strongest athletic/editorial character; least literal product explanation.

## QA and boundaries

- All text is deterministic and legible in the full-size exports.
- No third-party marks, fake product UI, medical/anatomical imagery, outcome claims, product-availability claims, or user/traction claims appear.
- These assets must not be published, used in ads, or represented as tested without explicit founder approval.

## Founder decision — 2026-07-11

- **Selected:** B — Distorted-object translation. It is the strongest/clearest visual and most directly communicates AGym's messy-to-usable transformation.
- **Secondary input:** C — Motion-field editorial. Its deep-teal/shell/coral palette and abstract energy are useful, but the visual is not clear enough to lead the system.
- **Rejected:** A — Kinetic type/data rhythm. It is not clear enough; its palette may be revisited separately without restoring the composition.

Next: create placement-specific internal creative using B's transformation mechanism. One tightly controlled B-mechanism + C-palette exploration may be made as a visual variation, but it must preserve product clarity and remain separate from message learning.

## Follow-up requirement — training legibility

Founder feedback after the board: the selected B visual is clear as a transformation, but it does not yet make training sufficiently obvious. All next B-system assets must carry at least two integrated training anchors: plain-language training copy, a compact training-log token, a non-branded movement/exertion cue, or a training-specific reality-to-context progression. See `docs/design/DESIGN.md` for the binding implementation rules.
