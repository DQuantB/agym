## 2. Daily plan (~45–60 min, founder; agent preps the night before)

| Slot | Time | Task |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | 10 min | Send 2–3 outreach messages (discovery or follow-up) from agent-drafted queue |
| Morning | 5 min | Check yesterday's funnel numbers + replies (agent digest) |
| Midday | 15 min | Reply-first engagement: 2–3 helpful replies on X, 1–2 comments on Reddit (warm-up), Discord participation |

| Evening | 5 min | Log anything learned → agent files it in prospect log / activity log same day |

Agent daily: draft queue, triage replies, update logs, monitor analytics/paid, flag anything needing a founder decision.

## 4. Monthly plan

### Month 1 (now → mid-Aug): validate demand
- Deliverables: live demo page; ≥15 discovery conversations; Show HN + IH launched; ≥100 unique visitors; funnel data vs thresholds; paid probe (if gated in); **beta go/no-go memo**.
- KPI focus: demo-start ≥30%, demo→signup ≥40%, ≥40% AI-for-fitness users.

### Month 2 (mid-Aug → mid-Sep): beta + presence
- Deliverables: first cohort onboarded (10–30); invite→activation rate; 2 build-in-public updates with real usage learnings; Reddit account clears posting bars in all GO subs; first subreddit posts done; waitlist keeps filling from continuous content.
- KPI focus: activation (first confirmed event) ≥50% of invited; ≥1 organic inbound signup/day by month end.

### Month 3 (mid-Sep → mid-Oct): iterate + scale decision
- Deliverables: beta iteration shipped from feedback; case-study-style content from real usage (permissioned); decision memo — scale spend (Reddit ads), Product Hunt launch, or reposition; updated strategy doc v2.
- KPI focus: retention of beta users (week-2 return rate), cost per qualified signup if paid resumed.

## 5. Social presence program (founder, cold-start)

Goal: not follower count — **credibility with ~200–500 of the right people** (AI-native fitness users, QS crowd, indie builders). Conversations > impressions.

### X content pillars (rotate; 2 posts/week + daily replies)

1. **Build-in-public AGym** — what got built, what broke, real decisions (like this marketing memory system itself; meta-content about agent-run marketing is itself interesting to the ICP).
2. **The pain** — concrete stories of AI fitness plans failing without context; screenshots of the re-explaining problem.
3. **Data ownership / local-first opinions** — why your fitness memory should be yours; short, opinionated takes. This is the tribe-building pillar.
4. **Own training log, dogfooded** — real messy log → AGym confirmed context, shown honestly. Proof-of-use without traction claims.

Rules: no engagement-bait, no fake traction, no AI-sounding voice (drafts must pass as the founder), claims stay inside `waitlist-smoke-test-v1.md` guardrails.

### Reddit presence (Weeks 0–3 = karma building)

- 5–10 genuinely useful comments/week in target subs; answer logging/AI-context questions properly.
- No AGym mentions until (a) account clears the sub's age/karma bar and (b) a GO rating exists.
- Long-term: become a recognized helpful account in 2–3 subs rather than posting drive-bys in ten.

### Discord / communities

- 3–5 servers; introduce honestly ("building a fitness data tool, here to learn"); help first, share demo only where rules allow.

### Monthly presence targets (hypotheses, revisit monthly)

- M1: X 50–100 followers incl. 10+ ICP-relevant; Reddit account eligible to post in ≥2 GO subs; 5 recurring conversation partners.
- M2: 150–300 followers; first subreddit posts net-positive; 1–2 small collabs (podcast/newsletter mention, mutual build-in-public exchange).
- M3: 300–500 followers; inbound DMs about AGym happening without prompting; one piece of content that clearly outperformed (study why, repeat).
