# Experiment — AGym performance-interface editorial v2

Status: internal creative preparation only. No publishing, spend, landing-page activity, user data, or performance data.

## Question

Can a performance-interface editorial visual system better express AGym’s tech-savvy fitness-data/context positioning than the retired manga direction, while keeping the M1 message constant?

## Hypothesis

A real post-training logging moment plus one compact, attached training-context card will make AGym feel more credible and relevant to technical fitness users than generic AI/dashboard or comic visual language.

This is a design hypothesis. It is not yet a live experiment or evidence of preference.

## Constants

- M1 paid message: `Stop re-explaining your fitness history to AI.`
- Same product boundaries and claims guardrails.
- Same intended audience: people already using AI for training, nutrition, or self-improvement.
- Same eventual conversion event: completed waitlist signup.
- Same requirement for a founder-approved, tested landing page/form before publishing.

## Visual variable

- Prior internal visual system: black-and-white instructional manga — retired by founder before publication.
- Current internal visual system: performance-interface editorial, specified in `../../design/DESIGN.md` and `../brand-system.md`.

## QA evidence

Both finished exports were reviewed for:

- readable deterministic typography with no missing glyphs;
- tech-savvy performance-editorial fitness direction;
- clear physical relationship between training scene, notebook, and context card;
- restrained single signal color treatment;
- absence of logos, readable fake UI, medical/anatomical/biometric content, body transformation imagery, and coaching/medical claims.
