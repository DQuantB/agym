# Coach Discovery Sprint v1

Status: draft research protocol. Created 2026-08-01. No outreach sent, no interviews conducted, no public content published, no contact data collected, and no evidence recorded at creation.

## Decision to inform

Determine whether independent online strength/hypertrophy coaches have a repeated, costly problem reconstructing client reality from messy check-ins—and whether a reviewable client-context briefing is a credible narrow future-prototype wedge.

## Hypothesis

Coaches with 10–50 online clients receive training and adherence context across fragmented channels. Before changing a plan, they manually reconstruct what happened, potentially slowing review and obscuring uncertainty. Some may use their own LLMs to prepare analysis, but need better structured, provenance-aware context.

This is a hypothesis. It is not a statement of product availability, performance, or market demand.

## First ICP

Independent online strength/hypertrophy coaches who manage roughly 10–50 clients and run recurring check-ins through messaging, forms, spreadsheets, or coaching platforms.

### Excluded from Sprint v1

- clinical rehabilitation / physiotherapy workflows;
- medical professionals or medical claims;
- dietitian/nutrition prescription workflows;
- broad gym management software buyers;
- coaches whose only interaction is in-person and who do not use recurring between-session check-ins.

## Method

Follow `../coach-discovery-operator-guide-v1.md` for the public-source scan rubric, qualification score, founder-review queue, exact no-link messages, and manual sending protocol.

1. Research up to 30 public-source prospects and relevant communities.
2. Founder reviews five no-link discovery openers at a time.
3. Founder personally sends 3–5 approved messages per day.
4. Use the interview guide to learn the current workflow before describing a solution.
5. Do not send a demo link until a coach confirms relevant pain and the founder approves the follow-up.
6. Log notes in an anonymized evidence ledger; preserve quote status and evidence strength.
7. Review after 15 completed conversations.

## Approved research questions

- What does a recent client check-in look like in practice?
- Where do training, adherence, food, recovery, energy, or discomfort notes arrive?
- What manual work happens before a coach decides whether to adjust a plan?
- What fails in the current workflow?
- Is the pain recurring enough to affect response quality, speed, or capacity?
- Does the coach already use an LLM privately, and what context does it lack?
- What privacy/trust conditions would be required for any future briefing workflow?

## Boundaries

- Founder sends every message, comment, or follow-up.
- No automation, mass outreach, scraping, private-group access, or hidden data collection.
- Do not request identifiable client data. If a coach voluntarily gives an example, ask them to anonymize it; do not commit it to the repository.
- No prototype claims beyond a clearly labeled future concept.
- No public promotion, email collection, ads, or spend in this sprint.

## Validation gate after 15 conversations

Proceed to a coach-prototype review only if all of the following are supported by the ledger:

- 8+ coaches describe a recurring messy-check-in/context-reconstruction problem;
- 5+ walk through a recent concrete case or anonymized example;
- 3+ agree to a future-prototype follow-up after hearing a precise workflow concept;
- 2+ articulate a credible potential payment rationale.

If any criterion is missed, write a decision memo that explains whether to narrow the segment, reframe the problem, change the research method, or stop.

## Metrics

Primary metrics are evidence counts, not follower counts, clicks, or signups:

- researched high-fit prospects;
- founder-approved messages;
- messages sent by founder;
- meaningful replies;
- completed conversations;
- validation-gate evidence counts;
- repeated workflow/pain themes;
- explicit objections;
- prototype follow-ups accepted.

## Founder approval needed before execution

1. Confirm the first ICP: independent online strength/hypertrophy coaches, 10–50 clients.
2. Approve the first five prospect records and no-link messages.
3. Approve any move from discovery to prototype explanation, demo link, or follow-up.
