# AGym Meta Ads v1 — creative handoff

Status: internal draft. Assets are production-ready files but are not approved for publication.

## Strategic recommendation

Run the first test as a **message test**, not a design contest.

- Keep the same black-and-white instructional-manga visual system across all variants.
- Use one audience hypothesis: people already using AI for training, nutrition, or self-improvement.
- Send every click to one landing page and one waitlist form.
- Hold the form and landing page constant while testing the first-line message.
- Optimize initially for completed waitlist signups, then check signup quality via the interest-track response and optional free text.

The v1 control message is deliberately broad and avoids suggesting AGym gives training, nutrition, or medical advice:

```text
Stop re-explaining your fitness history to AI.
```

## Control ad setup

### Image text

```text
AGYM
STOP RE-EXPLAINING YOUR FITNESS HISTORY TO AI.
MESSY LOGS > CONFIRMED CONTEXT
JOIN THE WAITLIST
```

### Primary text

```text
Your workout notes, pasted plans, and “I’ll remember later” context are scattered across apps and chats.

AGym is building a local-first way to turn messy fitness logs into clean, user-confirmed context you can bring to the AI tools you already use.

Join the early waitlist.
```

### Headline

```text
Your fitness memory layer for AI
```

### Description

```text
Local-first. Browser-based. Early access.
```

### Recommended platform CTA

```text
Sign Up
```

Use Meta’s native CTA button. The CTA printed inside the creative is visual reinforcement, not the functional button.

## Next two message variants — same visual system

Do not create more visual styles until the control generates enough signal to judge the message. Reuse the same asset composition and change only the copy.

### Variant B — cleaner logging

```text
Headline in creative: MESSY LOGS IN. CLEAN CONTEXT OUT.
Meta headline: Turn workout notes into AI-ready context
Primary text: Paste the messy version. Review the draft. Keep the raw log. Export context your agent can use later.
```

### Variant C — reality vs. plan

```text
Headline in creative: YOUR PLAN IS NOT YOUR REALITY.
Meta headline: Give your AI the context it is missing
Primary text: AGym helps you preserve what actually happened after the plan: workouts, notes, changes, and uncertainty — in a format you can review and reuse.
```

## Guardrails

- Do not claim that AGym improves outcomes, makes training safer, gives advice, diagnoses problems, or replaces a coach/clinician.
- Do not imply that a viewer has a health condition, poor fitness, or a personal failure.
- Do not use body transformation imagery, before/after imagery, weight-loss promises, or clinical/anatomical illustrations.
- Keep the `AGym v0 is being built` and `not medical software` disclosure on the landing page near the waitlist form.
- Do not use ChatGPT, Claude, Meta, or other third-party logos in creatives.

## Launch gate

Before publishing any ad, confirm:

1. A real landing page is live and its native CTA leads to a functioning form.
2. Form submissions are received and stored securely.
3. Privacy policy and v0/local-first disclosure are linked from the page.
4. Tracking and the definition of a completed waitlist signup are tested with a real test submission.
5. Founder approves budget, audience, geography, and the specific copy variant.
