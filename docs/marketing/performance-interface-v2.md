# AGym Performance-Interface Editorial v2

Status: internal-ready visual system application. No assets are published and no performance data exists.

Design source: `../design/DESIGN.md`

## Purpose

Apply the new tech-savvy, fitness-related performance-interface editorial direction to one paid M1 control and one organic explanatory post.

These assets test a new visual-system hypothesis. They do not establish that the M1 message or any visual direction performs better.

### Exact generated-background prompt

```text
Premium performance-interface editorial background for AGym, square 1:1. Photorealistic close dynamic crop immediately after a barbell set on a textured dark gym floor: a chalked adult hand rests near a barbell with visible knurling while the other hand makes a quick handwritten note in a small training notebook. Show subtle movement residue: a little chalk dust, notebook slightly displaced, no face and no physique focus. Deep near-black shadows with warm bone highlights, minimal editorial composition. Reserve a clean warm-bone negative-space block across the top 28% for headline typography. Embed one compact blank paper context card physically clipped to the notebook edge, with only abstract non-readable lines and a single small checkmark shape; it must feel physically attached, not like a floating software dashboard. No readable text, letters, numbers, logos, app UI, dashboards, robots, anime/manga, comic panels, glassmorphism, medical/anatomical/biometric imagery, body transformation, or stock-photo smile.
```

### Deterministic overlay treatment

- Ink headline only; no in-image CTA or secondary slogan.
- Signal lime `#C8FF00` only for one confirmation tick and one outward context/export mark attached to the card.
- Thin steel divider under the headline.

### Caption

```text
A training plan records intent. A confirmed log records what actually happened.

That distinction matters when you want your training history to remain useful beyond one note, one chat, or one app.

AGym is exploring a user-controlled fitness context layer: capture the messy reality, review it, and keep the confirmed context portable.
```

### Exact generated-background prompt

```text
Premium performance-interface editorial background for AGym, vertical 2:3. Photorealistic post-run track-side moment: close crop of a worn running shoe, water bottle, and an adult hand writing a short note in a small notebook beside a track lane line; a loosely placed towel or untied lace implies real movement. Favor objects and hands over face/body. Natural athletic realism with deep ink shadows, warm bone highlights, subtle grain, restrained contemporary editorial art direction. Reserve a clean warm-bone negative-space block across the top 25% for headline typography. Add one compact blank context card aligned physically with the notebook edge and a thin technical rule connected to the lane line; only abstract non-readable lines and one small checkmark shape, not a screen. No readable text, letters, numbers, logos, app UI, dashboard, robots, anime/manga, comic panels, glassmorphism, medical/anatomical/biometric imagery, body transformation, or influencer pose.
```

### Deterministic overlay treatment

- Ink headline only; no logo lockup, CTA, or subhead.
- Electric blue `#4C7DFF` only for one context/confirmation mark and one outbound directional mark physically anchored to the card and track line.
- Thin steel divider below the headline.

## Guardrails

- AGym is a user-controlled data/context layer, not an AI coach or medical product.
- Do not claim results, advice, training safety, diagnosis, treatment, performance optimization, product availability, or traction.
- No third-party logos, faces/physique focus, medical/biometric imagery, anime/manga, generic SaaS dashboards, or generated readable UI.
