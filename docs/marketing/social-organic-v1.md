## Caption

```text
A plan is not a record of what happened.

The useful context is usually scattered across workout notes, changed plans, missed sessions, and things you meant to remember later.

AGym is being built around a simpler loop:

messy log > user review > confirmed context

Log once. Own your data. Bring it to your agent.
```
