# AGym Waitlist Smoke Test v1

Status: draft for Orchestrator/founder review. Do not publish without approval.

## Objective

Validate which entry point creates the strongest demand for AGym among AI-native fitness users:

1. Messy Log Cleaner
2. AI Plan Tracker
3. Coach Briefing Generator

This is one AGym waitlist test with three interest tracks, not three separate products.

## Core positioning

AGym helps your AI coach understand what actually happened.

AGym turns messy fitness logs, AI-written plans, and real-world training data into clean, user-confirmed memory for ChatGPT, Claude, or your own agent.

Important boundary: AGym is not an AI coach and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment suggestions, or training/nutrition recommendations.

## How it works

```text
1. Paste or import
   Add a messy fitness log or an AI-written plan.

2. Confirm the data
   Review the draft. Correct what matters. Keep the raw text preserved.

3. Export context
   Generate an AI-ready briefing or JSON export for ChatGPT, Claude, or your own agent.
```

## 7-day launch plan

### Day 0 — Setup

- Choose landing page surface: Carrd/Framer/Tally, or static page in AGym repo.
- Create waitlist form with the fields above.
- Add basic analytics if available.
- Do not run paid ads yet.

### Day 1 — Founder post

- Publish one founder-led post explaining the problem.
- CTA to waitlist.
- Track replies and form submissions.

### Day 2 — AI-native communities

- Share a non-spammy version in relevant AI/productivity communities where allowed.
- Ask for feedback, not just signups.

### Day 3 — Fitness/logging communities

- Test the messy-log angle in fitness/logging/quantified-self contexts.
- Avoid sounding like medical advice or a transformation product.

### Day 4 — Direct discovery

- DM or message 10–20 relevant people personally.
- Ask what they currently use and whether re-explaining context is painful.

### Day 5 — Variant test

- Change hero headline or post angle based on early signal.
- Keep form questions stable.

### Day 6 — Optional small paid test

- Only after founder approval.
- Budget: 20–50 EUR maximum.
- Test one or two best-performing angles.

### Day 7 — Review

- Count signups by selected interest track.
- Read qualitative answers.
- Decide whether to keep current MVP emphasis or adjust next issue priority.

## Metrics

Primary:

- landing page visitors
- signup conversion rate
- selected interest track
- quality of free-text answers
- percentage already using ChatGPT/Claude for fitness

Secondary:

- ad/post click-through rate
- cost per signup if paid
- comment/reply quality
- current workaround frequency

## Decision rules

Strong signal for Messy Log Cleaner:

- highest selected track
- users mention scattered notes, logging friction, or re-explaining workouts
- current MVP remains priority

Strong signal for AI Plan Tracker:

- users mention ChatGPT/Claude plans they never operationalize
- interest track wins clearly
- keep Issue 3 scaffold, but consider moving plan-intake earlier after logging foundation

Strong signal for Coach Briefing Generator:

- users already have logs but need clean AI context
- emphasize briefing/export earlier in landing page and product copy
- still build the shared data foundation first

Weak signal:

- low conversion
- vague free-text answers
- users expect a full AI coach
- users do not currently use AI for fitness
- revise positioning before building too far

## Claims and safety guardrails

Do not say:

- AGym is your AI coach
- AGym gives fitness advice
- AGym diagnoses or treats pain/injury
- AGym optimizes health
- AGym guarantees results
- AGym has users/traction it does not have
- AGym is medical-grade

Safe language:

- AGym organizes user-confirmed fitness data.
- AGym creates AI-ready context.
- AGym helps users bring their own data to ChatGPT/Claude.
- AGym is local-first in v0.
- AGym does not provide medical advice.
